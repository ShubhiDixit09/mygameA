SIMPLE GUIDE

How to Play me vs me

🎯

1. The Goal: Score 7 Points to Win

Score by hitting the ball into your opponent's goal. The first player to reach 7 points wins the match!

🕹️

2. Controls & Super Smash

Player 1 (Blue) • Move: Mouse or W A S D • Super Smash: Left-Click or Space
Player 2 (Green) • Move: Arrow Keys • Super Smash: Enter

💡 Tip: Click or press Space right when the ball touches your striker to launch a lightning-fast Super Smash!

🧲

3. Watch the Magnets (Klask Rule!)

There are 3 white magnets floating in the center of the field. If you get too close, they magnetically stick to your striker.

⚠️ Penalty: If 2 magnets stick to your striker, your opponent automatically gets 1 free point!

⚡

4. Ball Speeds Up Each Volley

With every hit between players, the ball speeds up (+5 km/h). Fast rallies can reach speeds above 160 km/h!

🎮

5. Game Modes

• Play vs AI: Solo practice match against a smart computer opponent.

• 2-Player (1v1): Two friends playing head-to-head on the same keyboard.

• Solo Both Hands: Control both Blue and Green yourself to test your brain coordination!
