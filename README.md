# ME VS ME ⚡
### *The 3D Kinetic Mirror Duel (Klask & Windjammers Revived)*

> **🌐 Live Deployment: [https://mygame-one-theta.vercel.app/](https://mygame-one-theta.vercel.app/)**  
> *"You are your own worst enemy. Face your Parallel Reflection in the Zero-G Hyper-Arena."*

---

## 🌌 Overview
**ME VS ME** is an ultra-fast, addictive 3D arcade web game reviving the lost dexterity mechanics of physical bar classic **KLASK** combined with **WINDJAMMERS** and **TRON LIGHT-DISC DUELS**. 

Built with **Three.js WebGL**, procedural **Web Audio API** synthesis, dynamic lighting, real-time particle physics, and a clean modern obsidian HUD.

---

## 🎮 Controls

### **Player 1 — ME [ORIGIN PRIME] (Cyan)**
* `W` / `A` / `S` / `D` : Navigate Chassis
* `SPACEBAR` : Kinetic Deflector Shield (Time perfectly on hit for **PERFECT REFLECTION**)
* `Q` / `E` : Curve Slice / Magnus Spin

### **Player 2 — ME [SHADOW ALTER] (Magenta)**
* `Arrow Keys` : Navigate Chassis
* `ENTER` or `NUMPAD 0` : Kinetic Deflector Shield

---

## ⚡ Core Rules & The Klask Revival

1. **The Quantum Core (The Disc)**:
   - Every consecutive volley accelerates the core (+5 km/h per hit) up to supersonic speeds (>150 km/h).
2. **Magnetic Quantum Mines (Klask Hazard)**:
   - 3 floating magnetic mines drift in the center.
   - If you get too close, they magnetically latch onto your chassis.
   - **Magnetic Overload Penalty**: If **2 mines** stick to your ship, an overload detonates and the opponent automatically scores a point!
3. **Perfect Deflection**:
   - Pulse your shield right as the disc impacts to launch a supersonic rebound and instantly charge 40% of your Super Meter.
4. **First to 7 Points Wins**!
